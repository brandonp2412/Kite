Kite is a fast, native Matrix messaging client for Android and desktop.

Features include encrypted messaging, multi-account support, a unified inbox, rich-text messages, media attachments, reactions, and configurable keyboard shortcuts.

Matrix is an open, decentralized messaging protocol: you choose your homeserver or host your own. Kite is a community fork of SchildiChat Revenge and builds on the Matrix Rust SDK.

Kite is still under active development. Some Matrix features are not yet implemented.

Source: https://github.com/brandonp2412/Kite
Upstream: https://github.com/SchildiChat/schildi-revenge
