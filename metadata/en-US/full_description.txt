Kite is a fast, native Matrix messenger for Android and desktop. Keep your conversations together in a clean, responsive interface built with Kotlin/Compose and the Matrix Rust SDK.

• A unified inbox for direct chats and group rooms
• End-to-end encrypted conversations on supported Matrix rooms
• Multiple Matrix accounts and homeservers
• Quick reactions, replies, and swipe-to-reply
• Media attachments, files, and rich-text messages
• Search for chats and messages
• Appearance, notification, and conversation preferences
• Native desktop support and keyboard shortcuts

Matrix is an open, decentralized messaging protocol. Use a compatible homeserver or host your own.

Kite is a community fork of SchildiChat Revenge. The application remains in active development, and some Matrix features are incomplete.
